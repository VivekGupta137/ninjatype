# NinjaType — YouTube Shorts promo

A 30-second vertical promotion video using genuine NinjaType screenshots, animated typography, keyboard accents, gentle camera movement and an original instrumental score.

## Upload files

- `ninjatype-youtube-short.mp4` — main export with music.
- `ninjatype-youtube-short-silent.mp4` — identical visuals without audio, for adding music in YouTube.
- `thumbnail.jpg` — vertical cover image. YouTube Shorts cover selection varies by upload method; select a suitable video frame if image upload is unavailable.
- `storyboard.jpg` — quick visual overview of the seven scenes.

Both MP4 exports: 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps, 30 seconds, H.264 with web-friendly fast start. Main export has stereo AAC audio. Main messages are embedded in the picture and work without sound.

## Assets and editing

Product screenshots were captured from NinjaType on 28 October 2026. Screens are cropped for focus, not redrawn. This is an animated product promo assembled from screenshots, not a continuous recording of a live race. Displayed scores are sample session UI, not promised results.

`original-soundtrack.wav` is a synthesized score created for this video without third-party recordings or samples. `build_short.py` regenerates the video and previews using Pillow, FFmpeg, the original captures in `../promotion/raw/`, and the macOS Avenir Next font.

No video has been uploaded or published to YouTube.

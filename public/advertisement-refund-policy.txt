NINJATYPE ADVERTISING - REFUND POLICY
=====================================

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains when a payment for advertising on NinjaType
(ninjatype.com) is refunded, how much is refunded and how it is paid. It
covers the Always-visible sponsorship (the footer strip and the Results
spotlight, sold together as one plan) and sponsored blog reviews.

This policy is a plain summary of the refund rules in the advertising
Terms & Conditions at ninjatype.com/advertisement-tnc.txt (the "Terms").
It is part of the Terms and uses the same words with the same meanings.
Clause numbers below refer to the Terms. If this policy and the Terms
differ, the Terms prevail. It gives you no refund right that the Terms do
not give.

This policy is for advertisers only. NinjaType is free to use and visitors
pay nothing, so there is nothing to refund to visitors.

THE SHORT VERSION
-----------------
  - Payments are final. A refund is due only in the cases listed in
    section 1.
  - Cancelling a running booking yourself does not give a refund.
  - If we end a running sponsorship and you have not breached the Terms,
    you get a pro-rata refund for the unused days.
  - Every refund has a processing fee of 5% of the total amount you paid
    for the booking deducted from it.
  - Taxes and bank, card or currency charges are not refunded.
  - Refunds are paid in USD, by card, through a payment link or checkout
    that you provide. They are not sent back to the original payment
    method.
  - If you breached the Terms, no refund is due.

1. When a refund is due
   A refund is due only in these cases:
   1.1 You cancel before the ad is live. If you cancel in writing before
       the ad has gone live and before we have started work on it, we
       refund the payment, less the processing fee and a reasonable charge
       for work already done (clause 7.2).
   1.2 We end a running sponsorship. If we suspend or terminate a running
       placement and you have not breached the Terms, we refund the fee
       for the unused days, pro rata on the amount you actually paid for
       that booking. If you prefer and we agree, you can take a credit or
       an extension instead (clause 12.2).
   1.3 We change the Terms. If a change to the Terms materially reduces
       what you paid for and you do not accept it, you can cancel by email
       within 14 days of the change and we refund the fee for the unused
       days, pro rata (clause 13.3).
   1.4 A long outage outside our control. If an event outside our
       reasonable control keeps a placement offline for more than 14
       consecutive days, we extend the booking by the time lost or, at
       our choice, refund the unused days pro rata (clause 16).
   Each of these is your only remedy for the situation it covers. We owe
   no compensation, damages, lost profit or costs on top of it.

2. When no refund is due
   No refund, credit, extension or price reduction is due for any of the
   following:
   2.1 You cancel a booking after the ad has gone live, or after we have
       started work on it. Cancelling stops the placement or any renewal
       only (clause 7.1).
   2.2 You stop using a quarterly or yearly booking early. These prices
       are discounted for paying the whole term in advance and are not
       repriced (clause 4.7).
   2.3 You want to drop the footer strip or the Results spotlight alone.
       They are sold only together (clause 4.9).
   2.4 You breached the Terms, your ad, product or conduct exposes us to
       legal, security or reputational risk, or there is a payment dispute
       or chargeback. This applies whether the ad is paused, removed,
       restored or the booking is ended (clauses 12.3 and 12.4).
   2.5 Days lost while an ad is paused or removed until you fix a
       violation. The booking period keeps running and ends on its
       original end date (clause 12.4).
   2.6 Days lost because your assets arrived late or were not usable. The
       booking period starts on the 8th day after payment whether or not
       the ad is live (clause 5.3).
   2.7 The number of advertisers in a space, your share of the rotation,
       or how often your ad is shown. Every space is shared and rotating
       (clauses 2.4 to 2.7).
   2.8 Impressions, clicks, conversions, sales, rankings, traffic or any
       other result, or a difference between our figures and yours
       (clause 10).
   2.9 How the ad looks, once you have approved a preview or paid after we
       sent one (clause 5.6).
   2.10 Short interruptions, maintenance, visitors who block ads or
       disable scripts, and changes to the site's design or layout
       (clauses 2.2 and 5.4).
   2.11 A fault on our side that keeps your ad off the site for more than
       24 consecutive hours. The remedy is an extension of the booking by
       the time lost, not a refund (clause 5.4).

3. Sponsored blog reviews
   3.1 A review is non-refundable once we have started testing the product
       or writing (clause 7.3). Before that, section 1.1 applies.
   3.2 The service is complete when the review is published. Later edits,
       de-indexing or removal do not give rise to a refund (clause 12.5).
   3.3 A negative or mixed review, or a review you do not agree with, is
       not grounds for a refund or removal (clause 11.3).
   3.4 A review and a sponsorship are separate bookings. A refund for one
       does not affect the other (clause 4.5).

4. How the amount is worked out
   4.1 Pro-rata refund. Unused days are the days left in the booking
       period after the day the placement ends. The refund before fees is:

           amount paid for the booking x unused days / days in the booking

       A monthly booking has 30 days, a quarterly booking 90 and a yearly
       booking 360. Days are whole calendar days in UTC (clause 4.2).
   4.2 Processing fee. Every refund, whatever the reason for it, has a
       processing fee of 5% of the total amount you paid for the booking
       deducted from it. The fee is 5% of the whole payment, not 5% of
       the refund. If the fee is more than the refund, no refund is paid
       and you owe nothing further (clause 7.4).
   4.3 Taxes and charges. A refund does not include any tax, duty or
       withholding that was deducted, withheld, collected or paid in
       connection with the booking (including sales tax, VAT, GST and
       TDS), or any bank, card or currency-conversion charge. Recovering
       those amounts from a tax authority or bank is your responsibility
       (clause 7.5).
   4.4 Amount paid. Refunds are worked out on the amount we actually
       received for the booking, less any refund already given.

   Examples (prices as listed on 25 November 2026, before any tax):
     a) Quarterly booking, 199 USD. We end it after 30 of 90 days and you
        have not breached the Terms.
          Unused days:      60
          Pro-rata amount:  199 x 60 / 90 = 132.67 USD
          Processing fee:   5% of 199     =   9.95 USD
          Refund paid:      132.67 - 9.95 = 122.72 USD
     b) Monthly booking, 99 USD. We end it after 29 of 30 days.
          Unused days:      1
          Pro-rata amount:  99 x 1 / 30   =   3.30 USD
          Processing fee:   5% of 99      =   4.95 USD
          Refund paid:      none, because the fee is more than the refund
     c) Monthly booking, 99 USD. You cancel in writing before the ad is
        live and before we have started work on it.
          Processing fee:   5% of 99      =   4.95 USD
          Refund paid:      99 - 4.95     =  94.05 USD, less a reasonable
                            charge for any work already done
   The examples are illustrations. The Terms decide the amount in every
   case.

5. How to ask for a refund
   5.1 Email vivekgupta@ninjatype.com from the address used for the
       booking. Include the brand name, the booking (plan and term), the
       date and amount of the payment, and the reason for the request.
   5.2 For a refund under section 1.3, your email must reach us within 14
       days of the change to the Terms.
   5.3 Contact us before raising a dispute or chargeback with your bank or
       payment provider. If a payment is reversed, disputed or charged
       back, we may remove the ad immediately, no refund is due, and you
       owe the booking amount plus any fees we are charged (clause 6.4).

6. How refunds are paid
   6.1 Refunds are not sent back to the original payment method. To
       receive a refund you must give us, at your own cost, a working
       payment link or checkout on a payment network of your own that
       accepts card payments. We pay the refund by card through it. Any
       fee that network charges is yours to bear (clause 7.6).
   6.2 We are not obliged to pay by bank transfer, cheque, wallet or any
       other method, and no refund is due until you have provided the
       payment link or checkout.
   6.3 Refunds are paid in USD. Any difference caused by exchange rates
       is yours to bear.
   6.4 A refund is a full and final settlement of all claims about that
       booking (clause 7.7).

   shows the current version. Changes apply as set out in clause 13 of
   the Terms.

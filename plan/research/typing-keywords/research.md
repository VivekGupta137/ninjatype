# Typing race and multiplayer typing keyword research

Research date: 24 October 2026. Audience: global users searching in English. Product: NinjaType.

The strongest primary keyword in this niche is **typing race**. Use **typing test race**, **typing race game**, and **online typing race** as supporting phrases. For a live multiplayer product, **multiplayer typing test** and **typing race with friends** communicate the intended activity clearly, but this research does not establish large exact-phrase search volumes for them.

This conclusion combines live Google Trends comparisons, Google autocomplete, seven Google search-result samples, four competitor pages, and a review of NinjaType's current race implementation. It supersedes a priority list based only on autocomplete: frequent suggestions show useful vocabulary, but do not prove high search volume.

## Evidence and interpretation

- 50 autocomplete requests produced 183 unique suggestions. Four core seeds were sampled in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India; additional seeds were sampled in the US and India.
- Eight Trends comparisons cover worldwide Web Search, all categories, using English **search terms**, over five years and/or twelve months. Seven use unquoted terms; one uses quoted phrases to check ambiguous matching. The interface language is English; this does not filter the searchers' language or country.
- Each twelve-month comparison contains 52 complete weekly observations plus an incomplete week. Five-year comparisons contain 260 complete weeks plus an incomplete week. Calculations below exclude incomplete weeks.
- Seven Google result samples used `hl=en`, `gl=us`, and `pws=0` in an existing browser session. These are US-oriented snapshots, not a worldwide ranking audit. Browser/account context can still affect results.
- Competitor pages were inspected without entering races or creating accounts. Their feature descriptions are first-party observations, not independently verified usage statistics.

Trends indices are normalized from 0–100, not monthly searches. Unquoted multiword terms include their words in any order, with other words in between. Quoted terms match the phrase, with possible words before or after; they still are not a paid-tool exact-match keyword-volume report. Rounded zero values, `<1`, and insufficient data can obscure small terms. Google's documentation also warns that small queries can show statistical noise. Do not use isolated spikes or ratios between tiny indices as precise demand estimates.

No Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, Search Console, CPC, or keyword-difficulty dataset was available. This report therefore supplies evidence-backed priorities and intent, rather than invented monthly volumes or difficulty scores.

### Long-term movement and seasonality

The focused five-year race comparison is more informative than the broad chart because it avoids rounding the race term to approximately one point.

| Search term | First 52 complete weeks: mean index | Last 52 complete weeks: mean index | Relative change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| typing race | 72.42 | 62.40 | -13.8% |
| typing test race | 13.19 | 8.56 | -35.1% |
| typing race game | 7.10 | 8.48 | +19.5% |
| typing competition | 5.73 | 12.21 | +113.1% |

These percentages describe changes in normalized search interest, not absolute search counts. They are endpoint-window comparisons, not a claim of steady year-by-year growth. In particular, competition's first versus last quarter of the latest year declined in this sample; its longer-term increase does not establish ongoing acceleration.

Across the five-year race series, the average index for July was 50.4, June 54.4, September 76.3, and October 75.0. This is consistent with a recurring summer dip and stronger autumn interest. Nitro Type's recent-year June/July dip provides another directional signal of education-related seasonality. The data does not prove that schools cause the pattern, and worldwide school calendars differ.

For content releases, August–October is a reasonable planning window. Compare Search Console performance with the same months of the prior year before calling a summer decline an SEO failure.

### Product fit today

In the inspected code, `src/pages/online.astro`, `OnlineKeyboardContainer`, and the online FAQ describe racing recorded attempts from local typing history. They do not establish live multiplayer or private friend rooms. This limits which pages can satisfy social search intent today.

For the current mode, keep the primary race keyword while naming the actual activity:

- Suggested title: **Typing Race — Beat Your Past Attempts | NinjaType**
- Suggested H1: **Typing Race: Beat Your Past Attempts**
- Suggested description: **Practice in a free online typing race against your recorded attempts. Choose a 15, 30, or 50-word track and improve your WPM and accuracy.**
- Helpful explanatory copy: **Race against the recorded pace of your previous runs. Each attempt gives you a new pace to beat.**

The existing page title already largely follows this approach. A generic race query may still prefer live games; clear page copy improves expectation fit but does not guarantee rankings.

If live multiplayer and friend rooms ship, an appropriate title becomes **Typing Race With Friends — Free Multiplayer Typing Test | NinjaType**, with **Race Friends in a Multiplayer Typing Test** as the H1. Use it only when both promises are true. The feature should expose public play and create/join room actions immediately.

### Content opportunities

1. **TypeRacer alternatives:** explain which tools support live opponents, solo practice, word lists, quotes, accounts, statistics, and private rooms. Alternative-oriented pages and Reddit already appear in this query's results. Distinguish NinjaType's recorded races clearly.
2. **How to race friends online:** publish when NinjaType has actual invitation support. Include practical room creation and joining instructions.
3. **Typing races versus typing tests:** explain how races affect practice, WPM, accuracy, and text difficulty. Link to the relevant playable modes.
4. **Typing without punctuation:** provide a working mode and explain what its score measures. The sampled results favor dedicated practice/test pages.

Avoid creating a separate page for every spelling or word-order variant. Use related wording naturally in the title, visible introduction, mode descriptions, and relevant FAQs. Keep the playable activity easy to reach.

## Validation and measurement

The next quantitative step is a global English Keyword Planner export for the curated shortlist, supplemented by country-level US/UK/India checks. Record the selected geography, language, dates, matching behavior, and whether close variants are grouped. CPC and ad competition are advertising measures, not organic ranking difficulty.

In Search Console, group queries with an expression such as `(typing.*race|race.*typing|multiplayer.*typ|typ.*multiplayer|typing.*competition)`. Keep a separate friend subset such as `(friend|friends)`. Review query/page impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position. Once analytics are available, connect landing-page visits to race starts and completed races; for a future live mode, track room creation and successful friend joins.

Compare at least eight to twelve weeks after meaningful page changes, while accounting for seasonality and small samples. A term that does not appear in Trends can still be valuable if Search Console and completed-race data show qualified visits.

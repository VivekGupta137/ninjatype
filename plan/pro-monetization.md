# NinjaType Pro

Status: Proposed. Not started. Needs accounts and billing first.

Free stays free: all tests, lessons, themes, streaks, Ghost Race, multiplayer, sharing, and local history.

## Pro features (first paid release)

1. **Adaptive training:** a daily routine built from your target and time budget, with weak-key drills and weekly benchmarks.
2. **Weekly review:** a progress report comparing like-for-like tests, showing weak keys, plateaus, and next week's focus.
3. **History sync:** sign in and sync results, settings, lessons, and training across devices (12 months of detailed history).
4. **Pro themes:** new Pro-only themes, a Pro badge, and a custom theme builder. Existing themes stay free.
   - Builder: pick the theme colors (`--bg-color`, `--alt-bg-color`, `--main-color`, `--text-color`, `--sub-text-color`, `--sub-alt-text-color`, `--error-color`, `--success-color`) with a live preview and a contrast warning.
   - Save, rename, and delete custom themes; they sync across devices with the account.
   - Import and export a theme as a small JSON file.
5. **Footer recognition and profile:** opt-in name in the footer strip linking to a public profile (see `plan/feature/pro-footer-recognition.md`).

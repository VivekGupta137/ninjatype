## Less distraction. More practice.

NinjaType is a free typing practice tool built around a simple idea: improving your typing should feel effortless to get started. Open a test, settle into your rhythm, and watch your speed and accuracy improve over time. No account required.

## Your practice stays close

Typing history and preferences are stored locally in your browser. You can practice without signing up, and manage your saved data from [Settings](/settings/). Using another browser or clearing site data can remove that local history.

## Open by design

NinjaType is created and maintained by **Vivek Gupta**, with help from the open source community. It is released under the MIT license, so you can explore the code, suggest improvements, or help build what comes next.

Built with Astro, React, TypeScript, Nanostores, and Apache ECharts, and hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

[Explore the source on GitHub →](https://github.com/VivekGupta137/ninjatype)

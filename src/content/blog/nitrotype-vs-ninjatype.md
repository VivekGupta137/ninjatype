**Nitro Type and NinjaType both turn typing practice into a game, but they suit different routines.** Nitro Type emphasizes competitive racing, collection rewards, and tools for teachers. NinjaType combines timed typing tests, finger lessons, a personal-best Ghost Race, and configurable races with friends.

If you are searching for **NitroType.com vs NinjaType.com**, start with what you want to do: motivate a class, practise a troublesome key, measure a repeatable test, or organize a race with friends. The most useful choice is the one that helps you keep practising accurately.

## When should you choose Nitro Type?

[Nitro Type](https://www.nitrotype.com/) is a good option to explore when a racing game and collection rewards help you return to practice. Its official site promotes cars, stickers, titles, and a shop alongside competitive typing.

For teachers, the [Nitro Type Teacher Portal](https://www.nitrotype.com/teachers) is a substantial difference. Nitro Type describes classroom management, performance reports, and controls over friend racing, team play, and custom usernames. Its site also states that player chat is unavailable. Review those controls when deciding whether the service fits your classroom.

A typing game can help you repeat familiar movements. Learning where a key belongs may still call for a slower lesson. Nitro Type itself distinguishes its practice game from Typing.com’s lessons, which it links from its site.

## When should you choose NinjaType?

Choose [NinjaType typing practice](/) when you want to control your test length and vocabulary, then examine WPM and accuracy together. Time mode offers **25 seconds, 45 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, and 5 minutes**. Words mode gives you a fixed passage instead of a fixed timer.

Use [touch typing lessons](/learn/) when your main difficulty is finding keys without looking down. The exercises separate index, middle, ring, and pinky fingers, so you can work on a smaller movement before returning to a full passage.

If racing your own best pace is motivating, [Ghost Race](/ghost-race/) projects your fastest valid saved WPM onto a fresh passage. Underlined characters show the shadow’s progress. You can chase that pace without arranging a group session.

For friends, open [Multiplayer Typing Race](/multiplayer-typing-race/), choose Race with friends, and create or join a named room. After the security check, configure the race in the lobby and share an invitation. Words mode ends at the first accepted completion; Time mode compares correct-character scores when the timer ends.

## Which game helps you improve typing speed?

There is no head-to-head learning study behind this comparison. Your routine matters: repeating rushed mistakes can make a score look busy without addressing the movement that slows you down.

Try this practice loop with whichever game you prefer:

1. Take a one-minute baseline and note both WPM and accuracy.
2. Identify one recurring mistake, such as a missed letter or extra space.
3. Practise that movement slowly before another test.
4. Play a few races to use the movement at a faster pace.
5. Retest with the same duration and text settings.

A five-minute test can help you notice whether accuracy falls as you tire. A 25-second test gives quick feedback, but one short result should not become your only measure of progress. Read [how to get better at typing](/blog/how-to-get-better-at-typing/) for a longer practice routine.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is NinjaType a Nitro Type alternative?

Yes, if you want a browser typing game with tests and races. It is a different product: NinjaType does not reproduce Nitro Type’s car collection or Teacher Portal. Choose based on the features you will use.

### Can I race friends on NinjaType?

Yes. Create a friend room, share its link or exact room name, choose settings in the lobby, and ready up. The [multiplayer guide](/blog/multiplayer-typing-race-with-friends/) explains the race formats and results.

### Are Nitro Type and NinjaType connected?

No. NinjaType is independent of Nitro Type and Teaching.com. To use Nitro Type, visit its official website.

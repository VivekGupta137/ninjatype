**TypeRacer and NinjaType both offer typing races with friends and other participants.** TypeRacer’s public game emphasizes racing through passages, including quotes and themed text collections. NinjaType’s type racer adds configurable word-count and timed races alongside solo tests, touch typing lessons, and a personal-best shadow challenge.

If you are comparing **TypeRacer.com vs NinjaType**, look at the text you want to practise and how you want the round to end. Finishing a passage first and typing the most correct text in one minute are different challenges.

## What is TypeRacer good for?

[TypeRacer](https://play.typeracer.com/) describes itself as a global typing competition with live opponents and passages drawn from books, movies, songs, anime, and other sources. Its public page includes a Quote of the Day, individual practice, and a “Race your friends” racetrack option.

That makes it a useful service to explore if you enjoy racing through quotations and themed passages. Quotes can require punctuation, capitalization, and less familiar vocabulary, depending on the text you select.

TypeRacer also shows themed universes, including Dictionary, Numbers, and Repeating Words. It is therefore more varied than a quote-only typing game. Check the current text selector to find the collection that fits your practice goal.

You can enter its page as a guest. The page states that guest races are not saved and offers a free account for saving races and accessing detailed statistics. That is useful to consider if you want a persistent record of your sessions.

## What does NinjaType’s type racer offer?

[NinjaType Multiplayer Typing Race](/multiplayer-typing-race/) offers friend rooms with settings chosen in the active lobby. Everyone receives the same passage for that round.

Choose **Words mode** for a 15-, 30-, or 50-word finish-line challenge. The first accepted complete passage ends the race for everyone. A two-minute limit also ends a Words race if nobody finishes.

Choose **Time mode** for **25 seconds, 45 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, or 5 minutes**. Everyone has the same typing window. The server compares final correct-character scores when time runs out; accuracy breaks a score tie.

The dictionary changes the vocabulary. English 1k, English 5k, English 2k short and long, English 5k medium, and Brainrot are available. In timed races, a long passage loads before the start and the visible text advances with your typing. You do not wait for another text request between words.

You can also practise outside a group race. Use a [solo typing test](/) to measure a consistent duration, [Learn](/learn/) to practise finger movements, and [History](/history/) to compare completed sessions in the same browser.

## Which typing race should you use with friends?

Try TypeRacer’s friend racetrack when your group wants its passage collections and race format. Try NinjaType when your group wants to switch between a short word sprint and a shared timed window.

For friends with different speeds, a timed round gives everyone the same amount of practice. A word sprint can be useful when your group enjoys a clear finish line. Agree on the dictionary and format before judging scores.

To organize a NinjaType race:

1. Open [Multiplayer Typing Race](/multiplayer-typing-race/) and choose Race with friends.
2. Enter a nickname and room name, then complete the security check.
3. Configure Words or Time mode in the active lobby.
4. Share the invitation link or exact room name.
5. Ready up, race, and compare the final scores and performance chart.

See the [race-with-friends guide](/blog/multiplayer-typing-race-with-friends/) for more detail.

## Can you compare WPM between TypeRacer and NinjaType?

You can compare your experience, but treat scores from different test settings carefully. A familiar common-word list may be easier for you than an unfamiliar quotation. Duration, punctuation, corrections, and scoring rules can all affect the result.

NinjaType calculates WPM from correct characters divided by five, divided by elapsed minutes. Accuracy includes mistakes you later correct. To track improvement, repeat similar settings and review accuracy alongside speed. The [typing race practice guide](/blog/typing-race-improve-speed-accuracy/) explains how to turn races into targeted training.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is “type racer” the same as TypeRacer?

“Type racer” is also a general search phrase for typing race games. **TypeRacer** is the name of the specific service at [play.typeracer.com](https://play.typeracer.com/). NinjaType is a separate app with its own multiplayer typing race.

### Is NinjaType a TypeRacer alternative?

Yes, for browser-based typing practice and racing with friends. The [TypeRacer alternative guide](/blog/typeracer-alternative/) focuses on how to get started with NinjaType.

### Does NinjaType require an account?

No. Friend rooms use a nickname and a security check. Typing history is stored in your browser, so clearing storage or changing browsers can remove access to those saved results.

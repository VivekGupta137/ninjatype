**NinjaType Pro is an optional upgrade that keeps your progress in step across devices and points you at what to practise next.** It adds history sync, adaptive daily training, a weekly review and custom themes. Typing tests, lessons and races are free and stay free.

If you only want to take a test or race a friend, you do not need Pro. It is for people who practise regularly and want less guesswork.

## What stays free

Pro does not take anything away from the free app. These stay free for everyone:

- Typing tests, [touch typing lessons](/learn/) and races
- Your practice history on the device you used
- Key, finger and rhythm stats
- Practising your own material

You can [start a typing test](/) or [race your shadow in Ghost Race](/ghost-race/) with no account.

## What Pro adds

### History sync across devices

Without an account, your history lives on the device where you practised. With Pro, results, streaks and key stats follow your account, so you can start on a laptop and continue on a phone. It is useful when you want one honest record of your progress rather than separate partial ones. Your [history](/history/) page shows the combined view.

### Adaptive daily training

Generic drills treat every key the same. Adaptive training builds a short daily routine from the keys you miss or type slowly, and shows the evidence for each step so you can see why it was chosen. The plan is computed in your browser from your own history.

That makes it a good partner for accuracy work. If you are unsure where your errors come from, read [how to improve typing accuracy](/blog/improve-typing-accuracy/) first, then let your routine target the keys that need attention. Open [My training](/training/) to see it.

### A weekly review of like-for-like tests

Comparing a one-minute test with a five-minute test, or an easy word list with a hard one, tells you very little. The weekly review compares tests under the same conditions, so a change in your numbers is more likely to reflect your typing than the setup. If your speed seems stuck, it complements the advice in [how to break a typing speed plateau](/blog/typing-speed-plateau/).

### Custom themes

Pro lets you build your own colour theme, check its contrast, and import or export it as a file. Pro also unlocks the extra preset themes in the theme picker. Contrast warnings help keep text readable, which matters if you type for long sessions. Start in [Custom themes](/custom-themes/).

## Is Pro worth it?

Pro makes sense if you:

- practise on more than one device and want a single history;
- have a goal, such as a higher WPM or cleaner accuracy, and want a routine that adapts;
- like to track progress week by week without comparing unlike tests;
- want the app to match your setup and taste.

It is probably not needed if you test occasionally, only race for fun, or are happy with the free practice and lessons.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need Pro to use NinjaType?

No. Typing tests, lessons and races are free, with no sign-up needed to practise.

### What happens to my history if I cancel Pro?

Your account returns to the free plan and your history stays on each device where you practised.

### Does Pro make me type faster?

No tool does that on its own. Pro helps you spend practice time on the keys and habits that matter most, and shows whether your results are really improving. The improvement still comes from regular practice.

This policy explains how NinjaType handles information when you use its website, typing tests, lessons, races, optional accounts, the NinjaType Pro subscription, Hacker Typer, and Web Stories. NinjaType is maintained by Vivek Gupta. For privacy questions or requests, email [vivekgupta@ninjatype.com](mailto:vivekgupta@ninjatype.com).

## Information at a glance

- **You do not need an account.** NinjaType does not require an email address or password to practise or join a race.
- **Practice data is stored in your browser.** Settings, saved results, learning progress, and related statistics are kept locally. They do not sync between devices unless you turn on Pro history sync.
- **Accounts are optional.** If you create one, NinjaType stores your email address, display name, a protected form of your password, and sign-in session records.
- **Pro payments go through Dodo Payments.** NinjaType never receives your card details. It stores only your subscription status and the provider's reference numbers.
- **History sync is opt-in.** When a Pro member turns it on, practice results are copied to NinjaType's database so other signed-in devices can load them.
- **Online multiplayer uses a server.** Room details, participant information, race submissions, and chat are transmitted to run the shared experience.
- **Shared result cards are public by choice.** A result link contains the display name and score summary you choose to share.
- **Sponsor placements are contextual.** Sponsors do not receive your personal information from NinjaType, and placements are not targeted using your behaviour or profile.
- **Website visits involve service providers.** Hosting, security checks, analytics, and externally loaded resources can process request and device information.

## Practice data and browser storage

NinjaType uses browser storage to remember your theme and practice settings, completed test results, key-level practice statistics, lesson progress, and personal-best information. Results may include timestamps, test settings, speed, accuracy, errors, and duration.

Multiplayer preferences can include a nickname, avatar settings, and a randomly generated browser identifier. Temporary session storage can also hold connection details, your current race input, and progress-chart observations so a session can recover after a refresh or interruption.

The site may cache pages, scripts, images, and fonts through your browser and a service worker to make repeat visits faster. These caches are separate from your saved practice history. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see locally saved information.

## Accounts

An account is needed only for NinjaType Pro. When you create one, NinjaType stores:

- Your email address and the display name you enter.
- Your password in hashed form. The maintainer cannot read it.
- Sign-in sessions: a session token, its creation and expiry times, and the IP address and browser user-agent seen when you signed in. A session lasts up to 30 days and is renewed while you keep using the account.
- Short-lived request counters keyed to an IP address, used to limit repeated sign-in attempts.

Signing in sets a cookie that keeps you signed in. It is a necessary, first-party cookie, is not readable by page scripts, and is not used for advertising or analytics. Signing out ends the session.

Your email address is used to sign you in, to tie a subscription to your account, and to contact you about the account, billing, or a request you make. It is not used for marketing email and is not shared with sponsors. NinjaType does not currently send verification or password-reset emails.

The profile picture you choose on the account page, and the record of whether this browser last saw a Pro account, are stored in your browser only.

## NinjaType Pro payments

Pro subscriptions are sold by **Dodo Payments** as the merchant of record. When you start checkout, NinjaType sends Dodo Payments your email address, your display name, the plan you chose, and an internal account identifier so the payment can be matched to your account. You then enter payment and billing details on Dodo Payments' own pages. NinjaType does not receive or store card numbers, bank details, or your billing address.

Dodo Payments tells NinjaType about subscription events such as a payment, renewal, cancellation, failure, or refund. From these NinjaType stores your subscription status, the date your paid period ends, Dodo Payments' customer and subscription reference numbers, and a log of event identifiers and times used to avoid processing the same event twice. **Manage billing** opens Dodo Payments' billing portal through a one-time link.

Dodo Payments acts as an independent controller of the payment information it collects, including for fraud prevention, tax, and invoicing. Its own privacy policy, available on the [Dodo Payments website](https://dodopayments.com/), explains its practices. If you email about a refund, the details you send are used to handle it under the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy/).

## History sync

History sync is a Pro feature that is off until you turn it on in a browser. While it is on, that browser uploads your saved practice results to NinjaType's database and downloads results uploaded from your other devices. A synced result contains what is saved locally for a test: its identifier and time, the test settings, speed, accuracy, errors, duration, and the typing statistics recorded with it. The calendar days on which you practised are synced as well, to keep streaks consistent.

Synced history is linked to your account and is used only to provide sync. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with sponsors. Settings, lesson progress, custom themes, and training plans stay in your browser and are not uploaded. Adaptive training and the weekly review are calculated in your browser from your practice history.

Turning sync off in a browser stops uploads from it. **Clear cloud data** in [Settings](/settings/) deletes the synced copy from the database, and your other devices drop their copy the next time they sync. If Pro ends, sync pauses and the synced copy stays with the account until you delete the account or ask for it to be removed.

## Shared result cards

When you choose **Share result**, NinjaType puts your chosen display name, speed, optional accuracy, challenge, available test details, and card theme/avatar choices into a shareable URL. You can exclude accuracy before creating the link or hide it on the share page. Anyone with that link can read or copy the information. The link is encoded, not encrypted, and does not expire automatically. Copies of links, downloaded cards, or social posts cannot be revoked by clearing your typing history.

Cards and PNG downloads are generated in your browser. The display name is remembered locally for convenience. Opening a shared link sends its URL to the hosting service, and page URLs may also be processed by the analytics providers described below. If you send a card through another app or social platform, that service's own policies apply. Use a display name you are comfortable making public.

## Online races, spectators, and chat

When you create, discover, or join an online room, NinjaType processes information needed to connect participants and operate the race. This includes room names and settings, nicknames, avatar configuration, browser and participant identifiers, connection state, and security-verification tokens.

During a race, the service receives progress updates and submitted text and metrics used to validate results. If you use room chat, your message, nickname, and message time are sent to the service and shown in the room. Participants and permitted spectators can see shared room information, chat, and race results. Room listings may expose the room name and basic room details.

A room invitation or room name is not a guarantee of a private conversation. Use a nickname you are comfortable sharing, and avoid entering passwords, contact details, confidential material, or other sensitive information in names, chat, or typing fields. Other participants can copy or capture information you share.

## Hosting and security

NinjaType uses Cloudflare to deliver the website and operate multiplayer services. Like other web requests, visits and connections can expose an IP address, requested URL, browser and device information, request time, and technical error or security information to the hosting provider.

Online room entry uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish legitimate requests from automated abuse. The service sends the verification token and connecting IP address to Cloudflare for verification. Turnstile also processes browser and device signals as described in Cloudflare's policies.

These services support page delivery, connection recovery, score validation, reliability, and abuse prevention. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for its practices.

Account, subscription, and synced-history records are processed by NinjaType's account service on Cloudflare and stored in a database hosted by **Turso**. Connections to it are encrypted in transit. See [Turso's Privacy Policy](https://turso.tech/privacy-policy) for its practices. No online service can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure; use a unique password for your account.

## Analytics, cookies, and external resources

The main website loads **Simple Analytics** and **Google Tag Manager**, with **Google Analytics** configured through Tag Manager, to understand visits and use of the site. Measurement can include page URLs, referring pages, interaction events, approximate location, and browser or device characteristics. Requests to these providers also carry network information such as an IP address.

Simple Analytics describes its service as cookie-free analytics. Google Analytics can use cookies and identifiers to distinguish visits and measure activity. The presence of cookie-free analytics does not mean that every service used by NinjaType is cookie-free.

Aggregate visitor and page-view totals from Cloudflare's analytics are published on the [Advertise](/advertise/) page. These are site-wide counts and do not identify any visitor.

Some pages include a click-to-load YouTube video. The preview image is requested from YouTube's image servers when the page loads. The player itself loads only after you press play, from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (`youtube-nocookie.com`), and Google's policies then apply to it.

Web Stories load the AMP runtime from Google's AMP CDN. Some pages may request icons or other resources from external providers. Loading a resource makes a request to that provider, which can receive request and device information. The standalone Web Stories do not include the main site's analytics scripts.

Provider information and controls:

- [Simple Analytics Privacy Policy](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/privacy-policy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google's explanation of partner-site data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

The account and Pro pages load the same analytics as the rest of the site. Your email address, password, payment details, and synced history are not sent to the analytics providers by NinjaType.

Browser privacy settings, cookie controls, and content blockers can limit third-party requests or storage. Their effects depend on your browser and configuration. Blocking security scripts can prevent online room entry, and disabling browser storage can prevent preferences and progress from being saved.

## Sponsors and advertising

NinjaType may show sponsor placements (a sponsor card on the results screen and a one-line sponsor strip in the footer) and publish sponsored blog reviews. These placements are contextual:

- NinjaType does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with sponsors, and does not offer sponsors behavioural or interest-based targeting.
- The sponsor you see is chosen by a time-based rotation among the current sponsors, which is the same for every visitor at a given moment. It does not depend on your typing results, history, or profile.
- Sponsors are not allowed to load their own scripts, pixels, cookies, or trackers on NinjaType. Sponsor logos and images are served by NinjaType.
- Sponsors may receive only aggregate figures, such as total visits or clicks, that do not identify you.

If you click a sponsor link, you leave NinjaType. The link can carry campaign tags (for example UTM parameters) that tell the sponsor the visit came from NinjaType and from which placement. The sponsor's website then receives the usual request information, such as your IP address and browser details, and handles anything you do there under its own privacy policy.

If you book advertising, NinjaType processes the information you provide to handle the booking: your name, business and contact details, emails, the assets you send, and invoicing, tax, and refund details. Payments and refunds are handled by third-party payment providers; NinjaType's website does not collect or store payment-card details. Booking records are kept as long as needed to deliver the booking, resolve disputes, and meet tax and accounting obligations. A sponsor's name, logo, and link are public while a placement runs and may remain in published reviews and archived pages.

## How long information remains

Local preferences and progress can remain until you remove them, the app trims older records, or your browser clears or evicts its storage. Temporary session data follows the browser's session-storage behavior and the app's session cleanup. Browser session restoration may preserve some temporary data.

Multiplayer rooms are temporary and expire automatically. The application removes playable room state when a room closes; short-lived verification receipts and directory caches can remain during cleanup. This does not describe the retention of separate hosting, security, analytics, or email-provider records, which depends on those services and their settings.

Account records remain until you delete the account. Deleting it removes your profile, password, sessions, synced history, and subscription status from NinjaType's database. Expired sessions and rate-limit counters are removed as part of normal operation. The log of billing event identifiers does not contain your name or email and may be kept for bookkeeping. Dodo Payments keeps its own payment, invoice, and tax records for as long as its legal obligations require; deleting your NinjaType account does not delete them and does not cancel a subscription, so cancel in the billing portal first.

Support correspondence may remain as needed to handle the request, maintain a record of its resolution, or meet applicable obligations. Contact the maintainer if you want information associated with correspondence reviewed or deleted.

## Your choices and privacy requests

Use **Clear local data** in [Settings](/settings/) to remove the typing history and associated key statistics saved on this device. **Reset All Settings** clears local preferences and history. Your browser's controls for clearing NinjaType site data can additionally remove cookies, session storage, and cached resources. These actions can permanently remove your saved progress.

If you have an account, **Delete account** on the [account page](/account/) permanently removes it together with your synced history; practice stored in your browser is not affected. **Clear cloud data** in Settings removes only the synced copy. You can cancel Pro or update your payment method from **Manage billing** on the [Pro page](/pro/). To change the email address or display name on an account, or to receive a copy of the information held with it, email the maintainer.

Clearing browser data does not delete information already sent to a room, your account, a service provider, or an email recipient. Conversely, the maintainer cannot remotely recover or remove records held only in your browser.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Email [vivekgupta@ninjatype.com](mailto:vivekgupta@ninjatype.com) with your request and enough context to identify the relevant interaction. Reasonable verification may be needed to protect another person's information. The response will take account of applicable rights, technical limitations, and any legal obligation to retain information.

## Children and shared devices

NinjaType does not require users to provide their age or create an account. Accounts and Pro subscriptions are intended for people who can legally agree to the Terms of Service and make a purchase on their own; a child should use them only with a parent or guardian who creates the account and makes the payment. Sponsor placements must be suitable for a general audience and must not be directed at children or used to collect children's personal information. Parents, guardians, and educators should supervise children's use of shared rooms, chat, and external links, and follow any consent requirements that apply to them. Do not put a child's identifying information in a public nickname or chat message.

If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact the maintainer. On shared computers, clear local data when appropriate and avoid saving identifying nicknames.

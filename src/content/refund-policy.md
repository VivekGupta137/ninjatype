This policy explains when a payment for a NinjaType Pro subscription is refunded and how to ask for one. It is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service/). NinjaType is maintained by Vivek Gupta. Questions can be sent to [vivekgupta@ninjatype.com](mailto:vivekgupta@ninjatype.com).

Typing tests, lessons, and races are free, so there is nothing to refund for them. Advertisers are covered by the separate [refund policy for advertisers](/advertisement-refund-policy.txt), not by this page.

## The short version

- You can cancel Pro at any time. Cancelling stops future charges and you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.
- A first payment for Pro is refunded in full if you ask within 7 days of the charge.
- A yearly renewal is refunded in full if you ask within 7 days of the renewal charge.
- Monthly renewals are not refunded. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
- Duplicate or mistaken charges are always corrected.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method through Dodo Payments.

## How Pro is billed

Pro is a subscription sold as a monthly or a yearly plan. Prices are shown in US dollars on the [Pro page](/pro/) before you pay, and any applicable tax is shown at checkout. There is no free trial. The plan renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.

Checkout, billing, and refunds are handled by Dodo Payments, which sells the subscription as the merchant of record. Its name can appear on your card or bank statement and on your receipt.

## Cancelling

Open the [Pro page](/pro/) while signed in and choose **Manage billing**. This opens the Dodo Payments billing portal, where you can cancel or change your card. If you cannot reach the portal, email the maintainer from the address on your account.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not refund the current period. Pro stays active until the date shown on the Pro page, and after that the account returns to the free plan. Practice history already saved on your devices stays there.

Deleting your NinjaType account does not cancel a subscription by itself. Cancel the subscription in the billing portal first, then delete the account.

## When a refund is due

- **First payment.** If you subscribe to Pro and it is not for you, ask within 7 days of the charge and the payment is refunded in full. This applies once per person, to the first payment for Pro.
- **Yearly renewal.** If a yearly plan renews and you did not want another year, ask within 7 days of the renewal charge and that payment is refunded in full.
- **Duplicate or mistaken charges.** If you were charged twice for the same period, charged after a cancellation had taken effect, or charged an amount different from the price shown at checkout, the incorrect charge is refunded.
- **Pro did not turn on.** If you paid and Pro could not be activated on your account, and the problem cannot be fixed within a reasonable time after you report it, the payment is refunded.
- **Pro is discontinued.** If NinjaType stops offering Pro, or closes your account when you have not breached the Terms of Service, the unused whole months of a yearly plan are refunded pro rata. A monthly plan is simply not renewed.

When a payment is refunded, Pro ends on that account when the refund is issued.

## When a refund is not due

- A monthly renewal, or a yearly renewal asked about more than 7 days after the charge.
- The remaining days of a period after you cancel. Cancelling stops the renewal only.
- Not using Pro during a period you paid for.
- A feature changing, or a short interruption, maintenance, or an outage of the service.
- Losing access to your account, for example because of a forgotten password. Contact the maintainer for help with the account instead.
- An account restricted or closed for breaching the [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service/), such as cheating or abuse.
- A payment that has been disputed or charged back with your bank or card issuer.

## How to ask for a refund

Email [vivekgupta@ninjatype.com](mailto:vivekgupta@ninjatype.com) from the address on your NinjaType account. Include the date and amount of the charge and, if you have it, the receipt or payment reference from Dodo Payments. No reason is needed for a refund under the 7-day rules.

Please get in touch before raising a dispute or chargeback with your bank. A dispute takes longer than a refund, and Pro can be paused on the account while the dispute is open.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued through Dodo Payments to the payment method used for the purchase, in the currency of the original charge. NinjaType cannot pay a refund to a different card or account. How long the money takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer. Tax collected on a refunded payment is returned with it where the payment provider and the applicable tax rules allow.

Exchange-rate differences and any fees charged by your own bank are outside NinjaType's control and are not reimbursed.

## Your legal rights

Some countries give consumers cancellation or refund rights that cannot be waived, including a withdrawal period for online purchases. Nothing in this policy excludes or limits those rights. Where the law gives you more than this policy does, the law applies.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Pro changes. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. A change applies to payments made after it is published; a payment already made stays under the version in force on the day of the charge.

## Speed earns stars. Accuracy keeps them.

Finger lessons award one base star for every **2 WPM**, rounded down. Your accuracy determines how many stars are deducted. Your final total never drops below zero.

<div className="static-formula">floor(WPM ÷ 2) − accuracy penalty</div>

## Unlock finger levels

Each finger pair has **three levels**: home anchors, upper reaches, and lower reaches. Index training starts with **F and J**; the other pairs start with **D and K**, **S and L**, and **A and semicolon**.

Reach **10 stars and at least 95% accuracy in the same completed session** to unlock the next level. At 100% accuracy, 20 WPM earns 10 stars; at 95%, the one-star penalty means you need 22 WPM. Stars show your best session rating for the current level, rather than adding together across retries.

The next level starts at **zero stars** and adds keys while continuing to practise the earlier ones. Completed levels stay saved. Master all three levels for every finger pair to unlock TypeNinja.

## Accuracy matters

At **96% accuracy or higher**, you keep all your base stars. Below 40%, you earn no stars for that session.

<div className="static-table-wrap">

| Accuracy         | Stars deducted  |
| :--------------- | :-------------- |
| 96% and above    | None            |
| 86% to below 96% | 1               |
| 76% to below 86% | 2               |
| 66% to below 76% | 3               |
| 56% to below 66% | 4               |
| 46% to below 56% | 5               |
| 40% to below 46% | 6               |
| Below 40%        | No stars earned |

</div>

## Five stars. Five color tiers.

The display shows **five stars at a time**. Each new color marks another tier; the filled stars show your progress within it.

<div className="static-table-wrap">

| Total stars | Color tier |
| :---------- | :--------- |
| 1–5         | White      |
| 6–10        | Green      |
| 11–15       | Yellow     |
| 16–20       | Orange     |
| 21+         | Red        |

</div>

## Build accuracy before speed

Start at a comfortable pace, practice one finger at a time, and keep your eyes on the screen. Short, regular sessions help finger placement become second nature. Speed can follow once the motion feels familiar.

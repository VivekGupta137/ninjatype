**Renewal.** A subscription renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, at the price then in effect for your plan, until you cancel. **Cancelling.** You can cancel at any time by opening the Pro page while signed in and choosing **Manage billing**, which opens the payment provider's billing portal. Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, and the account then returns to the free plan.

**Failed payments.** If a renewal payment fails or the subscription is put on hold, Pro can end before a new payment succeeds.

**Refunds.** Refunds are covered by the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy/). In short, a first payment and a yearly renewal are refunded on request within 7 days of the charge, and other payments are not refunded except where that policy or applicable law says otherwise.

**When Pro ends.** Paid features stop working when Pro ends. Practice history already saved on your devices stays there and the free tools keep working. History sync pauses, custom themes can no longer be applied, and the copy of your history held with your account stays until you delete the account or ask for it to be removed.

**Changes to Pro.** Pro features may be added, changed, or removed as the product develops. If a change materially reduces what you paid for, or if Pro is discontinued, you can cancel, and the Refund Policy sets out what is refunded. Training routines and weekly reviews are generated from your own practice data; like the rest of the service, they are practice aids and not a guarantee of a particular result.

**History sync.** Sync copies your practice results between the devices where you sign in and turn it on. It is a convenience, not a backup service. Clearing synced history removes it from the account and from your other devices the next time they sync.

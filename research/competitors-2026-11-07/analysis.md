# NinjaType competitor research

Research date: 7 November 2026. Scope: free online typing tests, touch typing lessons, multiplayer typing races, typing games, and hacker-typer simulators. NinjaType's own routes used for overlap comparison: typing test (words/time/quotes), `/learn/` finger lessons, `/multiplayer-typing-race/`, `/ghost-race/`, `/history/`, `/hacker-typer/`, blog.

## Method

- Live Google SERPs (US, English, top organic results) for ten core queries on 7 Nov 2026: typing test, typing practice, typing games, learn touch typing, typing lessons for beginners, typing race, typeracer alternative, multiplayer typing race with friends, how to type faster, monkeytype alternatives.
- Each finalist's own homepage title/meta description read directly (Ratatype blocked by Cloudflare; SERP title used).
- Monkeytype popularity cross-checked on GitHub (monkeytypegame/monkeytype: 21,004 stars, 3,414 forks).
- Raw SERP observations: `serp-observations.json`.

Limits: SERP presence is a demand/visibility signal, not measured traffic; no paid traffic dataset was used. Rankings of the ten below are editorial judgments weighting feature overlap and SERP strength.

## Rising / adjacent competitors (not top 10)

- Race-focused: TypeRush, Typer.io (50+ player rooms, closest to NinjaType's room model), TypeRoyale (foony.com), TypingFastest, TypingTestGo.
- Lesson-focused: TypingStudy, AgileFingers, TypingAcademy, Sense-lang, Learntyping.org, How-To-Type.com, TypeQuicker.
- Content-arcade: TypingGames.Zone (155 games), FreeTypingGame.Net, Arcademics, ZType; TypeLit.io (type entire books).
- Hacker Typer niche (NinjaType's /hacker-typer/): hackertyper.net (SERP #1), hackertyper.com, GeekPrank, whitescreen.online prank screens, app-store clones. NinjaType already ranks on page 1 for "hacker typer" with its Geek Typer vs Hacker Typer comparison.
- Programmatic "alternatives" SEO entrants: typemasterai.com, typingfastest.com, typetimer.com, typequicker.com, typingtestgo.com, cosmickeys.app, sigiltype.com — all publishing "Monkeytype/TypeRacer alternatives" comparison pages targeting NinjaType's target terms.

## Gaps and implications for NinjaType

1. "Typing test" SERP leaders are old, ad-heavy sites (TypingTest.com, LiveChat) or Monkeytype; NinjaType does not yet surface in the top 10 for the head term — its page-1 presence is in multiplayer-race and hacker-typer long-tail.
2. No top-10 incumbent combines multiplayer rooms + ghost-race benchmarks + lessons + streaks in one free no-account product; the closest are Typer.io (rooms) and TypeRacer (races).
3. Programmatic alternatives pages are an emerging playbook worth matching with honest, evidence-based comparisons (as done for Geek Typer vs Hacker Typer).
4. keybr-style adaptive drill generation is the most visible feature gap versus the top 5.

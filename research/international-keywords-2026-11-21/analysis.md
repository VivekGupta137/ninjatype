# International keyword research — high-CPM countries

> **Correction (21 Nov 2026):** this first pass was knowledge-based. It has since been superseded on several points by live-data research in `deep-dive.md` (same folder) — notably: Swedish/Danish "blindskrift" means Braille (real terms: tangentbordsträning, maskinskrivning), German "Schreibgeschwindigkeit" is contaminated by hardware queries, Italian demand is driven by ATA/EIPASS certification, "test di battitura" has near-zero interest, and Key Hero is English-only. Use the corrected keyword table in `deep-dive.md` for all targeting decisions.

Research date: 21 November 2026. Scope: typing-test / touch-typing / typing-game keywords for the 14 target countries (US, AU, CH, NO, CA, UK, DE, DK, NZ, SE, NL, JP, SG, UAE).

## Method and limits

- Volume figures below are **estimate bands from category knowledge, not measured data**. They express relative market size for prioritization only. Before building content, validate every keyword with Google Keyword Planner, Search Console (existing impressions), and one SERP scrape per keyword. Follow the sourcing rules in `../typing-keywords-2026-10-26/faq-content-map.md` — no published volume is a traffic forecast.
- Competitor lists combine `../competitors-2026-11-07/analysis.md` (English SERP) with known local-language incumbents. Local incumbents marked with "verify" must be confirmed by a live fetch before a comparison page references them.
- Language coverage: English markets need no translation; the real work is German, French, Italian, Dutch, Norwegian, Danish, Swedish, Japanese, Arabic.

## 1. English markets (US, UK, CA, AU, NZ, SG)

No new locale needed. The existing site already targets the US keyword set; these countries share it. What matters is geo signals, not language:

- Add `hreflang` alternates: `en` (default), `en-US`, `en-GB`, `en-CA`, `en-AU`, `en-NZ`, `en-SG`, `x-default`.
- Canada: add French translations to capture Quebec (`fr-CA`); "perfectionnement à la dactylographie" is the Quebec office-skill phrasing.
- Singapore: English-only is correct. Chinese/Malay/Tamil typing are IME products — out of scope.
- Keyword opportunities per country are the existing English set: typing test, typing practice, typing games, learn touch typing, typing race, typeracer alternative (already mapped in `../typing-keywords-2026-10-26/targeted-keyword-map.md`).
- Volumes (bands, per country): US highest (typing test 100k+/mo band); UK, CA, AU roughly 1/10–1/5 of US each; NZ and SG ~1/20–1/50 of US. Same content ranks across them once hreflang and local search-console coverage exist.

Product prerequisites (blockers, see plan): Japanese test mode (show Japanese text + romaji, accept romaji input with built-in kana conversion), KPM metric alongside WPM, Japanese word lists, JP-friendly fonts.

## Prioritization summary (value vs. effort)

| Priority | Market | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Germany | Largest EU ad economy; straightforward Latin-script word list; strong keyword volume |
| 2 | France | Large volume, clear keyword set, one content variant also serves fr-CA |
| 3 | Japan | Highest CPM in Asia and huge typing-keyword volume, but needs JP input mode (product work first) |
| 4 | Netherlands | High English proficiency lowers risk; Dutch keywords have low competition |
| 5 | Italy | Medium volume, easy to serve from FR/DE playbook |
| 6 | Sweden → Denmark → Norway | Small markets, very high CPM, near-zero local competition; one Nordic playbook reused three times |
| 7 | UAE (Arabic) | Small volume but low competition and high CPM; RTL work required |
| 8 | English markets (US/UK/CA/AU/NZ/SG) | No translation — hreflang + GSC coverage + existing content; do the hreflang work immediately (cheap) |

Validation checklist before any content build: run each shortlisted keyword through Keyword Planner with the country filter, fetch the live SERP for the top queries, and confirm the local incumbents named above exist and match their description before writing comparison pages about them.
